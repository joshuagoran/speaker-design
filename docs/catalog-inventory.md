## Sources and limits

- US Speaker index pages read: 18", 15", 12", 10", 21", 6"/8", 2"/5", compression drivers, super tweeters. Plus bcspeakers.com triaxial list.
- No product pages were opened. Index pages carry no Thiele-Small columns, so the Thiele-Small rule was not applied (see section 4).
- Roles appear only where the index page names them.
- Prices are the US Speaker index price. "not on index" means the index shows none.
- Out of scope and left out: 2 in to 4 in drivers, horns, waveguides, crossovers, guitar and bass units, car and motorcycle units.
- "Faital Pro" PR names (for example 15PR400) are woofers. None of the pages list a passive radiator.
